# Hair Condition Rating Scale

To keep my observations consistent over time, I use a 1–5 scale for each hair condition metric.

## Dryness
1 = No noticeable dryness  
2 = Low dryness  
3 = Moderate dryness  
4 = High dryness  
5 = Very high dryness

## Softness
1 = Very low softness  
2 = Low softness  
3 = Moderate softness  
4 = High softness  
5 = Very soft

## Shine
1 = Very low shine  
2 = Low shine  
3 = Moderate shine  
4 = High shine  
5 = Very high shine

## Curl Definition
1 = Very low definition  
2 = Low definition  
3 = Moderate definition  
4 = High definition  
5 = Very high definition

## Breakage
1 = Very low / less than usual  
2 = Low  
3 = Normal  
4 = Higher than usual  
5 = Much higher than usual

These scores are based on personal observation and are used to track changes consistently throughout the project.
